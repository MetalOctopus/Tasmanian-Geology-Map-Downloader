## Map Series Information

The downloaded maps include:

1. **Digital Geological Atlas 1:25,000 Scale Series**
   - Detailed geological mapping at 1:25,000 scale
   - 245 map sheets covering Tasmania

2. **Geological Atlas 1:50,000 and 1:63,360 Series**
   - Regional geological mapping
   - 50 map sheets

3. **Digital Geological Atlas 1:250,000 Scale Series**
   - Large-scale regional geological synthesis
   - 5 map sheets covering entire state

4. **Statewide 1:500,000 Scale Maps**
   - State-wide thematic geological maps
   - 15 specialized maps (geology, mineral deposits, etc.)

5. **Mount Read Volcanics**
   - Specialized mapping of economically important volcanic belt
   - 17 maps at various scales

6. **Tasmanian Landslide Map Series**
   - Landslide hazard and geology mapping
   - 58 maps covering hazard-prone areas
